# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be a respectful, practical, and welcoming place for contributors, users, teachers, and maintainers.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still being precise about problems.
- Keep feedback focused on code, documentation, behavior, and user impact.
- Protect student privacy and avoid sharing real personal data in issues, commits, screenshots, or logs.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Publishing private information without permission
- Sharing credentials, production secrets, or real student data
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or commits that violate this code of conduct. Serious or repeated violations may result in being blocked from participation.
